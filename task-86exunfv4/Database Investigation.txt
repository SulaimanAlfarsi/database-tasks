Part 3 – Enterprise Decision Thinking
1. Why might a bank choose Oracle instead of MySQL?

Banks need strong security, high performance, and reliable systems. Oracle provides advanced security and enterprise support.

2. Why might a startup choose PostgreSQL instead of Oracle?

PostgreSQL is free and open source. It helps startups save money while still providing many powerful features.

3. Why do some companies stay with SQL Server?

Many companies already use Microsoft products. SQL Server works well with Windows, Azure, and other Microsoft tools.

4. How does cloud infrastructure affect database decisions?

Companies choose databases that work well in the cloud because cloud systems are easier to scale and manage.

5. Why do enterprise companies pay for commercial databases?

They pay for professional support, better security, regular updates, and guaranteed reliability.

Part 4 – Architecture & Scalability Thinking
1. Which DBMS is commonly used in enterprise systems?

Oracle and SQL Server are commonly used because they provide strong security and support.

2. Which DBMS is commonly used by startups?

PostgreSQL and MySQL are popular because they are free and flexible.

3. Which DBMS is commonly used in government sectors?

Oracle and SQL Server are often used because they meet security and compliance requirements.

4. Which DBMS is commonly used in cloud-native systems?

PostgreSQL and MySQL are widely used because they integrate well with cloud platforms.

5. Which DBMS is commonly used in banking systems?

Oracle is widely used because of its security and reliability.

6. Which DBMS is commonly used in large transactional environments?

Oracle and SQL Server are commonly used because they handle large numbers of transactions efficiently.

Part 5 – Migration & Industry Reflection
1. If most DBMS systems support similar features, why do companies choose different DBMS technologies?

Each DBMS has different strengths, costs, performance levels, and support options. Companies choose the one that best fits their needs.

2. Why do companies migrate from one DBMS to another?

They may want lower costs, better performance, improved security, or cloud support.

3. What technical challenges may appear during migration?

Data conversion problems, compatibility issues, and possible downtime can occur.

4. What business challenges may appear during migration?

Migration can be expensive, time-consuming, and may require employee training.

5. Why are migration projects considered high-risk?

There is a risk of data loss, system failures, or service interruptions.

6. How do database architecture decisions affect long-term software systems?

The database affects performance, scalability, maintenance costs, and future growth of the system.

Oracle Reflection
Why is Oracle heavily used in enterprise environments?

Oracle is reliable, secure, and designed for large business systems.

Why do banks and telecom companies often depend on Oracle?

Because they need high performance, strong security, and continuous availability.

What makes Oracle architecture different from traditional DBMS environments?

Oracle provides advanced features such as CDB, PDB, high availability, and enterprise-level management.
